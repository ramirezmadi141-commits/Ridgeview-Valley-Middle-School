About Ridgeview Valley

Our Mission, Vision & Community

Ridgeview Valley Middle School is dedicated to cultivating curious minds, resilient learners, and respectful community members.

Our Mission

To empower all students to achieve academic excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and demonstrate civic responsibility in a supportive, safe, and technologically enriched environment.

Our Vision

To be a premier middle school recognized for innovative teaching practices, exceptional student engagement, and a thriving school community where every Raptor soars.

School Improvement & PBIS Behavior Expectations

School Improvement Plan (SIP)

Our current SIP focuses on three primary pillars: 1) Enhancing literacy and numeracy across all content areas through targeted interventions, 2) Expanding STEM and computer science elective offerings, and 3) Strengthening social-emotional learning (SEL) support systems.

PBIS (Positive Behavioral Interventions & Supports)

At RVMS, Raptors abide by the ROAR expectations:

  • Responsibility: Be accountable for your actions and learning.
  • Ownership: Take pride in our school environment.
  • Attitude: Maintain a positive, growth-oriented mindset.
  • Respect: Treat peers, staff, and property with dignity.

Academics & Curriculum
